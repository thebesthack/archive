CARZANART

A futuristic city design based in Northern Austria, including transportation, water, energy, farming, and urban planning.

What was the project?

Our project was to create a sustainable city with residential, industrial, commercial, natural, and infastructure components

My idea

Our original idea in early stages of development was to make a city out of cardboard in a way that was not structured. I had a issue with making it better in the first place and after trial and error (6 attempts) we made the city.

The process

We started of with using paper and cardboard to construct houses and banks. We slowly went towards 3D prints and colored paper for more scale and neatness.

💡 What I learned: I learned from this project that in order to have a good project you need to make the project at least 3 times

The final result

Our final result was a city that was sustainable and eco-friendly. We had a lot of fun making it and we learned a lot about how to make a city that is good for the environment and can support population.
